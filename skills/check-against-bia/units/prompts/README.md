## Which task first?

Pick a task where all four answers are *yes*.

- Do you do it at least once a month?
- Is it a task you would rather not do?
- Could you explain the steps to a new colleague in a few minutes?
- Will a person still review and approve the result, and can the work carry on without the tool?

**Ask the AI4BCM Guidance chatbot which task to pick.** This will help you identify which section of the guide covers it. <https://ai4bcm.org/chat>

**Step 2**

## The prompt pattern

```
Role:    BCM analyst [context], working on [the larger task] for [who will use the output].
Task:    [one narrow, clear task].
Sources: the attached material only, as evidence about the organisation. Use no facts or figures from general knowledge.
Output:  [the structure you want, for example an executive summary, then one section per site].
Gaps:    anything the sources do not answer. Do not guess it; list it here. Also list any source that is a draft or out of date.
Cite:    document and section for each point. Put copied words in quotation marks.
```

**Six example prompts are online, ready to copy.** <https://ai4bcm.org/guidance>

## For even better output

1. Include a few examples of good output and mark them as such.
2. Place long source material first, each document in its own marked block, and place your question at the end.
3. After the first draft, ask the AI to find a supporting quote for each claim, and remove any it cannot support, marking where it was.

## Share your prompt with your team

A prompt becomes a team asset when it is saved and given a version number, an owner and a named reviewer. Before the team relies on it, run it on three past cases for which you know the correct answer.
