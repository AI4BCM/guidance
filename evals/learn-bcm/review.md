No row is weakened and none is missing, so no paragraph follows. No row is left to the person
either: the Onboarding section gives the assistant the whole of what this skill does, and the two
boundaries in its Review boundary cell are the control itself, carried at the top and again under
**Reviewer**, where the learner or a colleague checks the lesson and a case goes to the BC manager.

Two rows deserve a note on how they are carried rather than whether. The Output row's "marked where
the documents are silent" is carried as the fixed string "not in the sources", which the skill also
uses for a standard's requirement without the learner's licensed copy; the two silences read the
same to the learner, which is what the Govern Minimum controls want, since the conformity judgement
stays a person's "against the source text". And the planted-text rule is carried as a closed list of
the slots the text may not fill (an example, a claim, a question, an item under "Where your
organisation decides"), so the self-test questions and answers are covered by "a question" rather
than named separately.
