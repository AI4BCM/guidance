# Site A: chilled dispatch recovery plan

Version 3, 12 March 2026. Status: approved. Plan owner: Head of Logistics.

## 1. Scope

Loss of Hall 3 (chilled dispatch) for more than two hours. Recovery option: move chilled dispatch to Site B.

## 2. Recovery objective

Chilled dispatch resumes from Site B within 12 hours of the loss of Hall 3.

## 3. Staffing

A minimum of 3 pickers per shift travel to Site B.

## 4. Contacts

## 5. Suppliers

Trunking from Site A to Site B and outbound chilled transport: NordFrost. Backup carrier: KuehlTrans, by spot
booking.

## 6. Systems
