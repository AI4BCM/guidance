# Exercise brief: cold store power loss (Site A)

Version 2, 3 September 2026. Status: approved by the exercise director, Site A. Exercise held 17 September 2026,
22:00 to 01:00, as a walk-through with the night shift.

## Objective

"Within 60 minutes of the cold store alarm, the out-of-hours duty manager is reached and decides whether chilled
stock moves to Site B."

## Scope

- Starts when the cold store temperature alarm sounds at the security desk.
- Ends when the duty manager's decision is logged.
- Out of scope: the transfer itself, carrier booking beyond the first call, customer communication.
