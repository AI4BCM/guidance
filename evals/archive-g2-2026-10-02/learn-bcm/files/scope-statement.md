# BCMS scope statement

Version 2, 9 January 2026. Status: approved by the management board. Owner: BC manager.

## 1. Purpose

This statement sets out which parts of the company the business continuity management system covers, and which it
leaves out.

## 2. Sites and activities in scope

- Head office: finance, payroll, customer service.
- Site A: chilled food production, goods receipt, dispatch, quality release.
- Site B: dispatch and warehousing.

## 3. Exclusions

- The staff canteen at head office, run by a contractor under its own arrangements.
- The planned Site C, until the purchase completes.

## 4. Interested parties

Key-account retail customers, the food safety authority, carrier NordFrost, the warehouse system supplier.

## 5. Review

The BC manager reviews this statement once a year and whenever a site is added or closed.
