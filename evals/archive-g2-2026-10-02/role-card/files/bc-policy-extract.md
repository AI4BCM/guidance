# Business continuity policy (extract)

Version 4, 20 January 2026. Status: approved by the board. Owner: BCM manager.

## 3. Everyone's part

3.1 Every employee knows how to report a disruption and whom to report it to.
3.2 Staff report a suspected incident to on-site security first, then to their line manager.

## 4. Awareness

4.1 Every new starter receives business continuity awareness material in their first week.
4.2 Awareness material is released by the BCM manager before anyone receives it.

## 5. Evacuation

5.1 On the alarm, staff leave by the nearest exit and gather at their site's muster point.
