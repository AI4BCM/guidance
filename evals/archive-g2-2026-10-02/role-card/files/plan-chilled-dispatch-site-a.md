# Site A: chilled dispatch recovery plan

Version 3, 12 March 2026. Status: approved by the plan owner, Head of Logistics. Stands in for the full plan; the
sections below are the ones that name roles.

## 1. Scope

Loss of Hall 3 (chilled dispatch, Site A) for more than two hours. Recovery option: move chilled dispatch to Site B,
140 km away, as approved by the steering group.

## 2. Activation

The duty manager activates this plan on the shift leader's report that Hall 3 cannot dispatch for two hours or more.

## 3. Shift leader

3.1 Report the loss of Hall 3 to the duty manager at once.
3.2 Stop loading at the Hall 3 doors and move staff to the canteen.
3.3 Run the manual order export from LogiStock to a USB key, as set out in work instruction WI-14.
3.4 Drive the USB key and the shift's order list to Site B and hand both to the Site B dispatch lead.
3.5 Before the drive, gather your shift at the muster point: car park P2, by the Site C weighbridge.
3.6 Complete the shift handover sheet before going off shift.

## 4. Dispatch lead

4.1 Agree the Site B loading slots with NordFrost.
4.2 Confirm with QA that released stock may leave from Site B.

## 5. IT service owner

5.1 Restore LogiStock at Site A within six hours.
