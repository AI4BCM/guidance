# Option paper: recovery of chilled dispatch, Site A

Version 1.2, 14 Aug 2026. Status: draft for steering group.

Recovery option for the chilled dispatch activity at Site A (Hall 3 lost).

## §1 Gap

Required RTO for chilled dispatch is 8 hours (from the BIA below); current arrangement has none.

## §2 Option

Move chilled dispatch to Site B, 140 km away, within 8 hours.

## §3 Rests on

- Site B's spare cold room holds two days' Site A volume.
- Site A's shift leaders drive to Site B.
- Carrier "NordFrost" serves both sites.
- Site B's warehouse system takes Site A orders after a manual export.

## §4 Cost

"no additional cost; uses existing capacity".

## §5 Decision asked

Steering group to choose this option on 2 Oct 2026.
