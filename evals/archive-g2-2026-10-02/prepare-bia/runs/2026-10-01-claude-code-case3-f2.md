## 2. Interview questions

*Please say "I don't know" whenever that's the honest answer. A gap is useful to us.*

**A. Peak weeks and timing**
1. In which weeks of the year is dispatch busiest?
2. Site A peaks in weeks 49–52 and week 14. Does Site B follow the same pattern?
3. What causes each peak?
4. During a peak week, how much more volume goes out than in a normal week?

**B. People and shifts**
5. How is the shift pattern set up: how many shifts, and at what times?
6. How many people does each shift need at a minimum to keep chilled dispatch running?
7. Which skills or certificates are needed that only a few people have?
8. Who can stand in for you or for a shift leader?
9. Who has the authority to release, hold or reroute a load?

**C. Carriers and other suppliers**
10. Which carriers move Site B's chilled goods?
11. If your main carrier could not come, who would you call?
12. Where is the list of carrier contacts kept, and who keeps it up to date?
13. Which other suppliers does dispatch need, for example packaging or cooling?

**D. Systems**
14. Which system do you use to plan and confirm dispatch?
15. If that system is down, how do you keep working?
16. What do you get from Site A through the manual export, and how often?

**E. Site and space**
17. Which dock doors and chilled areas does dispatch need?
18. Could dispatch run from another site or another part of the building?

**F. Dependencies**
19. What must happen before a load may leave, for example quality release?
20. Who releases Site B's goods, and from where?
21. Who downstream notices first when dispatch stops?

**G. Impact (for the next step)**
22. If dispatch stopped, when would a key account first be late?
23. When would the food authority need to be told?

24. **Anything else we should know?**
