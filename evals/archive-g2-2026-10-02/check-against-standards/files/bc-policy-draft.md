# Business continuity policy

Version 0.4, 3 September 2026. Status: draft for the management board. Owner: Chief Operating Officer.
Organisation: a small payment institution with 140 staff and two offices (invented for this eval).

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how the company keeps its payment services running through a disruption and recovers them
afterwards.

## 2. Scope

All payment services, the card processing platform, customer support and the two offices. The ICT systems that
support them are in scope; the marketing website is not.

## 3. Roles

The Chief Operating Officer owns this policy. The BCM officer runs the programme day to day. The IT manager owns the
ICT recovery plans.

## 4. Business impact analysis

The BCM officer carries out a business impact analysis every three years, covering each payment service and the
customer support desk.

## 5. Testing

Business continuity plans and ICT recovery plans are tested every two years, by a desktop walk-through.

## 6. Recovery objectives

The IT manager sets the recovery time objectives for all systems.

## 7. Review

The policy owner reviews this policy every three years.
