**Method 1, weakened.** The unit's step 1 is "Fix the sources you will attach, and their dates. With memory, past chats, project files, connectors and web search switched off, what you do not attach is not in the answer. Where any of them is on, the model may draw on it without saying so. Whether the model filled the gap or left it out, the output looks the same." The skill keeps the first sentence and drops the condition the rest of the step states. Its step 1 fixes the sources and their dates and then spends the step on the prompt's Intake questions and on filing the organiser's answers as statements; no line in the body, and no step of `preflight.md`, asks the manager to switch memory, past chats, project files, connectors or web search off before drafting. Preflight step 4 is the nearest thing, and it is an instruction to the model ("Read only the attached files") rather than something the manager sets in the tool, so it does not reach a connector or a saved memory the model may use without saying so. A manager running the skill with any of those on would still get what looks like a clean draft built from the attached sites, and would have no way to tell a requirement drawn from an attached register from one the model carried over from a past chat — exactly the failure the unit's last sentence warns about, and the one that the skill's own citation of principle 3 under Sources rests on. Everything else the skill asks in step 1 is carried: the Intake question set and the "own statements, not sources" filing follow `units/prompts/bia.md` as written.
