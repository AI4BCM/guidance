Every row is carried, so no row needs a weakened or absent write-up. Four rows turned on a judgement rather than
a word match, and the reasoning is recorded here.

**Method 1.** The unit's step has two halves, the sources with their dates and the switched-off channels. The
switched-off half is carried in the unit's own five channels: the skill has the organiser confirm memory, past
chats, project files, connectors and web search in the same message, and a channel left on goes under Gaps. The
dates half is not spelled out in the step's body since the 2026-10-02 cut; it is carried by the bolded heading,
which quotes the unit's sentence whole ("Fix the sources you will attach, and their dates"), by the preflight the
first line runs ("Name each source's version and date" in one line per document at the top of the draft) and by
the Task choice's Output, kept, which opens with the source documents and their dates. A manager reading the step
fixes the dates; it is the thinner half of the two and worth watching in the next sweep.

**Method 2.** The unit's step carries a parenthesis — activities, resource requirements, questions, recovery
fields blank — that the body no longer repeats. Three of the four are in the Task choice the step tells the
manager to run, word for word from the prompt's Task line. The fourth is the one control at the top of the same
file, unconditional and read before the Method, plus preflight step 2 and the prompt's own Output. The text the
model actually receives says to leave the recovery fields empty, so the step runs the way the unit says.

**Method 3.** The unit addresses the analyst: use the draft as the thing to correct, not the thing to confirm.
This skill stops at the pre-interview draft, so the only way it can carry the step is to write the instruction
into the draft, and it addresses the activity owner. That is the right addressee: the owner's confirmation is what
puts an entry in the register, so the owner is the person whose rubber-stamping the step guards against (IMDA
section 2.2.2, in the skill's own source list for this rule).

**For the auditor.** The unit's paragraph has a register half and a risk half. The skill carries the register half
and names which three of those fields are necessarily blank before any interview has happened. The risk half is
out of scope by the unit's own words: this skill runs the BIA draft, and "The BIA method does not cover risk
assessment".
