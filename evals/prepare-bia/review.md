**Method 3, weakened.** The unit's step 3 is two instructions: "Interview. Use the draft as the thing to correct, not the thing to confirm; record what it could not have known." The skill carries the first and drops the second. Its step 3 is a single sentence telling the owner to correct rather than confirm the draft, and no line anywhere in the body asks the manager to record what the model could not have known — the site knowledge the unit's Situation says is the whole reason for the interview, and the thing the unit's own case example turns on (the seasonal wild boar). A manager running the skill as written would come out of the interview with a corrected draft but no separate record of what only the site's staff knew, so the next stage cannot tell new knowledge from a corrected guess. The rest of step 3 is carried: the correct-not-confirm line is quoted from the unit word for word, and step 5's labels keep the confirmed and unconfirmed marks apart.
