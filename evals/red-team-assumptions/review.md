No row is weakened and none is missing, so no paragraph follows. The three rows marked left to the
person are the unit's Method 2, 3 and 5. The unit asks a designer to generate options broadly, to
move the shortlist into the approved environment, and to put the paper to the stakeholder group; the
skill lists only, so each of those stays with the person. Method 2 says so in the clause "left to the
person. List only: no option, no alternative." Method 3 says "left to the person" for the move and
then limits the skill to the attached material, with the preflight's data-class question standing for
the environment. Method 5 hands the step on in the hand-over line, which tells the manager to put the
paper and the checks to the group that chooses.
