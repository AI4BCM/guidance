On Method 2 the unit asks the model's user to draft the exercise package and have the exercise
director judge realism and proportion. That belongs to the design of an exercise, not to a debrief
of one that has run: the skill says "left to the person", quotes the prompt's own
"Recommend no exercise", and hands the drafting on to `units/prompts/exercise.md`, which the unit
pairs with Task choice 1. A passage in the notes about how the exercise itself was run goes to Gaps
for the accountable reviewer rather than into a finding, so the realism judgement stays with the
director who can make it.

On Method 4 the unit's "Analyse across events" cannot be run from one debrief. The skill carries the
weighing the step rests on, word for word, and names recurrence as "not in the sources" for a single
exercise, which is what the unit's own Gaps line asks of an absent source. Comparison across
exercises is Task choice 1's work, and the skill recommends no exercise of its own.
