# Procedure: exercising business continuity plans

Version 1.2, 15 November 2025. Status: approved by the BCM officer. Owner: BCM officer.

## 1. Frequency

Each payment service's plan is exercised once a year.

## 2. Before the exercise

The exercise director writes the objective down and agrees it with the plan owner.

## 3. After the exercise

The exercise director writes a report within ten working days, naming each action and its owner.
