# Management reporting

```
Act as a BCM reporting analyst. Using only the approved KPI and validation data provided, draft a
concise management review narrative. Highlight trends, material gaps, progress on actions and
issues requiring leadership attention. Do not overstate assurance.
```

Without the last line you get a narrative that reads as reassuring when the data is not.
